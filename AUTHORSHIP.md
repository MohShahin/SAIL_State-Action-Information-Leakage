# Authorship and credit

This is a draft policy for discussion. It will be finalised after the team meeting.

- Papers follow ICMJE-style criteria: a substantial contribution to the design, analysis or writing;
  drafting or revising it; approving the final version; and accountability for the parts you did.
- Contributors who do not meet all criteria are acknowledged by name in the paper.
- Each paper has a named corresponding author and a short contributions statement.
- Contribution to the package, library, teaching material or translations is credited in release notes
  and on the website, whether or not it leads to authorship.
- Order and credit are agreed in writing before a submission, not after.
