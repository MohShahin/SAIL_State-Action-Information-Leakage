Datasets & Models

A place to flag datasets and models known or suspected to involve state-action leakage. There's no catalog here yet — this page states what exists today and how to contribute.

Know a dataset or model with this problem?

If a published dataset or model resembles one of SAIL's five leakage categories — or a pattern that isn't one of the five yet — file it through the structured submission form: Submit a dataset or model. It asks whether it's a dataset, a model, or a paired pipeline, a link, and the specific leakage concern.

This project's own dataset

SAIL's research was built on MIMIC-IV, a credentialed-access ICU dataset. What this repository can and can't publish about it — and why — is documented in DATA_ACCESS.md.

What's not here yet

A browsable catalog of submitted datasets and models is a later addition, once there are submissions worth displaying. Submitting one now is still useful: it's the first entry, not a message into a void — open submissions are visible on GitHub in the meantime.
