# Code of conduct

This project is a research community that includes students, clinicians, engineers and researchers
from many countries and languages. We want it to be a place where all of them can learn and contribute.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient; assume good faith; give specific, kind feedback; credit other
people's work; keep discussion about the work, not the person. Questions from beginners are welcome.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private
information, posting patient-level data, or presenting another person's work as your own.

**Reporting:** use GitHub's private "Report a vulnerability" form on this repository, or contact the
maintainers listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove content,
and may suspend or remove people from project spaces for conduct that breaks these rules.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
